Health questions: where to go (and where AI fits)

For symptoms, use NHS 111 online, the NHS App or call 111 (999 in an emergency). An AI chatbot can help explain a word on a letter or prepare questions for your GP, but it is not a doctor and can be wrong.

Getting started

  1. Go to 111.nhs.uk (or get the app) and open it. Both picks have a free way to start.
  2. Check the privacy setting shown above before you start chatting.
  3. Type your first request in plain English, for example:
    (Use NHS 111 online for symptoms.) Explain in plain English what 'HbA1c' on my blood test letter means and suggest 3 questions for my GP.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change ("shorter", "more formal", "add prices").

The example request is our suggestion, not a tested result.

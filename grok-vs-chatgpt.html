Questions

Is Grok better than ChatGPT?

It depends on the job. We pick Grok as best for: none of our jobs yet. We pick ChatGPT as best for: homework & learning help, making pictures, planning trips & meals, voice chat & a friendly companion, job hunting: CVs, cover letters & interviews, budgeting & money admin. We haven't tested them side by side ourselves yet; picks rest on dated public evidence.

Which is cheaper in the UK, Grok or ChatGPT?

Grok: SuperGrok Lite £10/mo; SuperGrok £30/mo (App Store). ChatGPT: Go £6.99/mo; Plus £19.99/mo (App Store). Check the maker's site before paying.

Which is better for privacy?

Grok: Yes, unless you switch it off (vendor FAQ). Opt-out: Yes: app Settings > Data Controls > Improve the model; grok.com Settings > Data; Private Chat not used for training. FAQ says EU/UK users can always opt out. ChatGPT: Yes on personal plans. Opt-out: Yes: Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone; Temporary Chats not used.

Can I use Grok and ChatGPT for free?

Grok: Yes (limits unknown). ChatGPT: Yes: unlimited text chats (abuse guardrails), limited uploads, images, voice and deep research.

Questions

Is ChatGPT better than Claude?

It depends on the job. We pick ChatGPT as best for: homework & learning help, making pictures, planning trips & meals, voice chat & a friendly companion, job hunting: CVs, cover letters & interviews, budgeting & money admin. We pick Claude as best for: writing emails, letters & CVs. We haven't tested them side by side ourselves yet; picks rest on dated public evidence.

Which is cheaper in the UK, ChatGPT or Claude?

ChatGPT: Go £6.99/mo; Plus £19.99/mo (App Store). Claude: Pro $20/mo, or $17/mo billed annually (USD, ex tax, vendor). UK App Store shows £20/mo (secondary). Check the maker's site before paying.

Which is better for privacy?

ChatGPT: Yes on personal plans. Opt-out: Yes: Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone; Temporary Chats not used. Claude: Yes, opt-out (vendor pricing table: 'Model training: Opt-out' on Free/Pro/Max). Opt-out: Yes: Settings > Privacy; Incognito chats.

Can I use ChatGPT and Claude for free?

ChatGPT: Yes: unlimited text chats (abuse guardrails), limited uploads, images, voice and deep research. Claude: Yes: chat on web, desktop and mobile; web search; voice; limited usage (vendor).

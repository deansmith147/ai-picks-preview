Questions

Is ChatGPT better than Copilot?

It depends on the job. We pick ChatGPT as best for: homework & learning help, making pictures, planning trips & meals, voice chat & a friendly companion, job hunting: CVs, cover letters & interviews, budgeting & money admin. We pick Copilot as best for: none of our jobs yet. We haven't tested them side by side ourselves yet; picks rest on dated public evidence.

Which is cheaper in the UK, ChatGPT or Copilot?

ChatGPT: Go £6.99/mo; Plus £19.99/mo (App Store). Copilot: Included in Microsoft 365 Personal £8.49/mo or £84.99/yr. Check the maker's site before paying.

Which is better for privacy?

ChatGPT: Yes on personal plans. Opt-out: Yes: Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone; Temporary Chats not used. Copilot: Yes for signed-in personal accounts, unless you opt out; not used when signed out, under 18, or inside Microsoft 365 apps (vendor). Opt-out: Yes: copilot.com profile > Privacy > Training on conversation activity / voice.

Can I use ChatGPT and Copilot for free?

ChatGPT: Yes: unlimited text chats (abuse guardrails), limited uploads, images, voice and deep research. Copilot: Yes: free in Windows, Edge and web.

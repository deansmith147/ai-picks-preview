Questions

Is ChatGPT better than Gemini?

It depends on the job. We pick ChatGPT as best for: homework & learning help, making pictures, planning trips & meals, voice chat & a friendly companion, job hunting: CVs, cover letters & interviews, budgeting & money admin. We pick Gemini as best for: none of our jobs yet. We haven't tested them side by side ourselves yet; picks rest on dated public evidence.

Which is cheaper in the UK, ChatGPT or Gemini?

ChatGPT: Go £6.99/mo; Plus £19.99/mo (App Store). Gemini: AI Plus £4.49/mo; AI Pro £18.99/mo; Ultra from £79.99/mo. Check the maker's site before paying.

Which is better for privacy?

ChatGPT: Yes on personal plans. Opt-out: Yes: Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone; Temporary Chats not used. Gemini: Yes while 'Keep activity' is on (the default); some chats are human-reviewed and kept up to 3 years (vendor). Opt-out: Yes: turn off Gemini Apps activity > Keep activity, or use temporary chats.

Can I use ChatGPT and Gemini for free?

ChatGPT: Yes: unlimited text chats (abuse guardrails), limited uploads, images, voice and deep research. Gemini: Yes: £0 with a Google account.

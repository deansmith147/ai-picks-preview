Glossary: AI words in plain English

Chatbot / AI assistant
A website or app you type (or talk) to, and it writes back. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are chatbots.
Prompt
What you type to the AI. Plain English works: say what you want, who it's for and how long.
Model
The 'brain' behind an app. One app can offer several models (for example a fast one and a smarter one). Leaderboards rank models, not apps.
Free tier / free plan
What you can do without paying. Usually limited: a number of messages, images or credits per day or month.
Credits
Points some tools use to measure usage. Each image, song or video uses some credits. When they run out, you wait for a refill or pay.
Subscription
A monthly or yearly payment for more use or better models. It usually renews automatically until you cancel.
Training on your data
Some companies use what you type to improve their future AI. Many let you switch this off in settings. We show where on each pick.
Opt out
Switching a setting off so something stops happening, for example so your chats aren't used for training.
Temporary / incognito chat
A chat that isn't saved to your history and, for the tools that say so, isn't used for training.
Hallucination
When an AI states something false confidently. Always check facts, names, numbers and quotes yourself.
Leaderboard (e.g. LMArena)
A public ranking where people compare two anonymous AI answers and vote for the better one. We cite these with their date.
Sourced only / Tested
Our labels. 'Sourced only' means facts come from linked pages; 'Tested' means we tried it ourselves with published prompts. Everything is 'sourced only' for now.

Best AI for summarising PDFs & videos

Upload PDFs, links or videos and ask questions about just those. Free ChatGPT handles a limited number of uploads.

Getting started

  1. Go to notebooklm.google (or get the app) and open it. Both picks have a free way to start.
  2. Check the privacy setting shown above before you start chatting.
  3. Type your first request in plain English, for example:
    (Upload your PDF first) Summarise this in 5 bullet points and list anything I need to act on.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change ("shorter", "more formal", "add prices").

The example request is our suggestion, not a tested result.

How much does Gemini Notebook cost?

Bigger limits with Google AI Plus/Pro/Ultra (see Gemini). Prices change, so check the maker's site before paying.

Does Gemini Notebook use my chats to train its AI?

Chats follow Gemini's Keep activity setting; uploaded source files are 'not used directly' for training (vendor) · Opt-out: Yes: turn off Keep activity.
